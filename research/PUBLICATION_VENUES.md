# Publication venue screen

Screened October 23, 2026. This is a fit assessment, not an acceptance prediction. The full requirements and unresolved actions are in [SUBMISSION_READINESS.md](SUBMISSION_READINESS.md).

## PennScience High School Journal

Closest near-term fit because it explicitly accepts original high-school-led engineering research and lists a November 1 annual deadline. The deadline is not currently safe to target until student eligibility, 2026 call status, its conflicting page-count/citation instructions, and AI-use policy are clarified. The LaTeX draft does not meet its template format, and the separate figure deck and permission form are not complete. It is the preferred venue to contact first, not a ready submission.

Official pages checked: [Guidelines](https://pshsj.org/guidelines), [FAQ](https://pshsj.org/faq), [Submissions](https://pshsj.org/submissions).

## Journal of Emerging Investigators

Not recommended. Its current published scope excludes computer science and papers that only introduce, optimize, or compare a model/method. The project is a simulator sensitivity study. Its current AI policy also prohibits several uses made in this workflow. JEI requires student and adult senior authors, adult submission, and its Word manuscript template; it charges a $49 submission fee with a waiver request path.

Official pages checked: [Guidelines](https://emerginginvestigators.org/submissions/guidelines), [Topics](https://emerginginvestigators.org/submissions/hypothesis-driven-research), [AI policy](https://emerginginvestigators.org/submissions/academic-honesty-and-ai), [Eligibility](https://emerginginvestigators.org/submissions/author-eligibility), [FAQ](https://emerginginvestigators.org/submissions/faq).

## Other options

No alternative student journal has been verified as a better target in this screen. Do not infer review quality, indexing, fee status, or eligibility from a venue name or third-party list. If PennScience cannot be resolved or its deadline is missed, conduct a fresh venue-specific check before submission.

JOSS is a research-software venue rather than a student journal. Its software and paper scope is not a fit for this empirical manuscript as written. A separate software paper would need to satisfy JOSS's current significance, documentation, testing, and scope requirements; it is not a near-term fallback.

## Archives are not journals

Zenodo, arXiv, and OSF Preprints can provide archiving or preprint distribution subject to their current rules. A deposit is not peer review or journal acceptance. No deposit has been made.
